Refund Policy

If you believe a charge related to the Yeti Crossroads Backpack offer was made in error, contact support at before opening a bank dispute. This helps us locate the transaction and resolve the issue quickly.

How to Request a Refund

Email with your full name, email address used at checkout, phone number, billing ZIP code, approximate purchase date, last four digits of the payment card, and a short explanation of the issue.

Review Timeline

We aim to acknowledge refund requests within two (2) business days. Most requests are reviewed within five (5) business days after we receive enough information to identify the transaction.

Refund Method and Timing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method whenever possible. After approval, the credit may take five (5) to ten (10) business days to appear on your statement, depending on your card issuer or financial institution.

Eligibility

Refund eligibility is reviewed case by case and may depend on transaction status, duplicate billing, payment processor rules, product access, support history, and fraud-prevention requirements. This policy does not limit any rights you may have under applicable law.
